## Problem

Researchers needed faster ways to locate trustworthy information across large paper collections.

## Why it matters

When exploration takes too long, students avoid deeper experiments and settle for shallow baselines.

## Approach

I designed the platform around three layers:

- Ingestion: parser and chunking pipeline with section-level metadata.
- Retrieval: sparse and dense hybrid ranking with reranking.
- Answering: grounded generation with source highlighting.

## Challenges

- Citation mismatch under partial context windows.
- Data freshness across weekly ingestion updates.
- Balancing latency with reranking quality.

## Results

Pilot users reported noticeably faster literature sweeps and higher confidence in generated summaries.

## Learnings

Evaluation design should be a first-class system, not an afterthought.
