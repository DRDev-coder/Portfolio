## Problem

Service failures were hard to diagnose because logs, metrics, and traces were disconnected.

## Why it matters

Reliability without observability is mostly guesswork under pressure.

## Approach

Introduced OpenTelemetry instrumentation, metric tagging conventions, and alert playbooks.

## Challenges

- Standardizing telemetry semantics across multiple service owners.
- Avoiding alert fatigue while preserving sensitivity.

## Results

Teams resolved incidents significantly faster and built stronger debugging habits.

## Learnings

Instrumentation quality matters as much as dashboard visual quality.
