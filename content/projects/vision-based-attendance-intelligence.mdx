## Problem

Instructors lost valuable teaching time to manual attendance workflows.

## Why it matters

Reducing administrative overhead creates more room for high-quality learning interaction.

## Approach

A face embedding model was optimized for edge inference with threshold tuning for robust decisions.

## Challenges

- Lighting variability across classrooms.
- Fairness and privacy constraints in deployment.

## Results

Classroom throughput improved while preserving transparency through audit logs.

## Learnings

Operational policies are as important as model metrics for real deployments.
